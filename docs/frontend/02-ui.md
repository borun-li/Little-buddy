- **Author:** fe-ui #1 · **Status:** draft

- **Inputs:** `01-ux.md` (fe-ux #1 draft, one-stop run), taste from `project-context.md` (visual / showcase, brand none yet, rich motion, warm copy)

- Brand: none yet. The palette and typefaces below are a proposal for the user's approval at the UI stop.

## 1. Design plan
### Palette
| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Oat | #F7F1E8 | page surface, text on Ember |
| Cocoa | #2F2724 | text, the companion's outline and features, focus ring |
| Linen | #E6DACA | 1 px borders, your turns, neutral glow |
| Ember | #A9442A | the talk button; the only loud colour |
| Honey | #F2B25C | the companion's fur; pleasant glow |
| Tide | #587489 | unpleasant glow, hint icon; the only cool colour |

### Type
| Role | Typeface | Size / weight / line height |
|---|---|---|
| Companion voice: replies, its caption | LXGW WenKai 霞鹜文楷 (OFL, bundled as a local subset, works offline) | 20 / 400 / 32 laptop · 18 / 400 / 28 phone |
| Your turns, input, hints | system sans: PingFang SC, Noto Sans SC, system-ui | 16 / 400 / 26 |
| Talk button | system sans | 17 / 600 / 24 |
| Small text: voice tag, privacy line, mood caption | system sans | 13 / 400 / 20 |

### Layout
- Laptop: the companion's stage takes the left 800 px; a 640 px log column sits right, behind one 1 px Linen rule. Only the log scrolls; the composer stays at its foot.
- Phone: the stage is pinned on top at 280 px (a third), the log fills the middle, the composer is pinned at the bottom.
- Density moderate; 4 px spacing scale; 6 px radius on every control.

### Principles
- The companion is the page: it is the largest thing on screen and never scrolls away.
- Two voices, two typefaces: the companion writes in WenKai with no bubble; you speak in plain sans inside a Linen box.
- Mood is expression first. Colour follows one rule: warm Honey for pleasant, cool Tide for unpleasant, stronger glow for higher energy. Movement comes third.
- Warm paper, thin lines, no gradients. Ember appears only on the talk button (and as the companion's blush).
- Privacy is felt, not announced: one quiet line in the empty state, no "offline" badge.

## 2. Self-check
| Read like a default | Replaced by | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Two-sided coloured chat bubbles | Companion replies as bubble-less WenKai text on a 2 px Honey rule; your turns in Linen boxes | The voice tells who speaks; the page reads like notes, not a messenger |
| Mood shown by a background colour wash or gradient | The face changes (eyes, brows, mouth, ears, paws); a flat glow disc behind it is tinted by the valence and energy rule | Colour alone fails colour-blind users and the "expression, not colour" rule |
| Calm-wellness purple and blue palette | Warm oat and cocoa paper with honey and ember; Tide is the only cool hue and it means unpleasant | Purple and blue gradients are a generic AI default; warm reads as a friend at night |
| Inter or one system sans for everything | WenKai for the companion's voice, system sans for yours | A second voice face gives the character a hand of its own |
| Small mascot avatar beside every message | One large companion pinned on the stage; no avatars in the log | The mood change must be seen together with the reply |
| Red tint and angry brows for unpleasant-high | Cool Tide glow, raised inner brows, a sweat drop, paws clasped at the chest | It must read worried with you, never angry at you |
| Pulsing red recording dot | The talk button stays Ember, turns pressed, shows seconds and three level bars | Red alarm feels like an error; holding to talk should feel safe |
| "Offline" badge or banner | Nothing; the privacy line lives only in the empty state | `01-ux.md` offline row: no notice |

### Plan revisions after drawing
- Focus ring moved from Ember to Cocoa: an Ember ring on the Ember talk button was invisible.
- Figma stand-ins: LXGW WenKai TC (the SC build is not in the Figma account) and Noto Sans SC Bold for the 600 weight. The page bundles LXGW WenKai (SC), whose punctuation sits low-left, not centred.
- Added `color.cocoa-55` for control borders: Linen on Oat is too faint for a field edge.
- DFB-001: the phone listening label "N 秒 · 松开发送" did not fit 124 px; the phone now shows icon, seconds and level bars only (FRAME-011).
- DFB-002: the paused input stays editable and focused instead of disabled, matching the `01-ux.md` loading row.

## 4. Components
Icons: Lucide, line style (mic, mic-off, info, lock). Figma: page 1, sheets FRAME-009 and FRAME-010.

### CMP-001 — Companion
- **Variants:** Mood = neutral, pleasant-high, pleasant-low, unpleasant-high, unpleasant-low (expressions in FRAME-009). Hooks: `data-testid=companion`, `data-mood`.
- **Poses (DFB-003):** every mood reads at thumbnail size by silhouette as well as face:
  - neutral: upright, round eyes, soft raised brows, ω mouth.
  - pleasant-high: jumps 30 px, arms raised, ^ ^ eyes, open laugh, five sparkles.
  - pleasant-low: leans 9° right, closed smiling eyes, paws folded on the belly, tail curled in front, small heart.
  - unpleasant-high: hunched and wide, wide white eyes, inner brows raised, wavy mouth, paws clasped, two sweat drops, tremble marks; worried with you, never angry.
  - unpleasant-low: sunk and flattened, ears drooping, big wet eyes, one tear, paws limp, tail flat.
- **Glow (DFB-003; §3 `color.mood.*` tokens and the Figma Primitives carry these values since D-004, 2026-11-14):** neutral Linen solid; pleasant-high Honey 64 %; pleasant-low Honey 30 %; unpleasant-high Tide 46 %; unpleasant-low Tide 30 %.
- **Parts:** each mood SVG names its part groups (§5); parts move per §7. Reduced motion keeps the drawn pose, so face, pose and glow still tell the mood.
- **Sizes:** laptop 540 px wide (stage), phone 240 px plus a 14 px caption.
- **States:** default (one per mood), focus (not focusable; no interaction), transition between moods (240 ms, §7). Accessible name "小伙伴，<心情>" via `role=img`.
- **Content limits:** caption "小伙伴 · <one phrase>", at most 8 characters after the dot. (English limit: Increment 1, CMP-001.)
- **Overflow:** none; the stage scales to fit its box, never crops the ears.

### CMP-002 — Turn
- **Variants:** you, you-voice (tag "语音 · 示例文字" with mic icon — tag retired, increment 1 → "语音" / "Voice"), reply, typing.
- **Sizes:** fills the log width (560 laptop, 350 phone); your box hugs its text up to 440 px laptop, 280 px phone.
- **States:** default, focus (not focusable; the log is a `role=log` live region), loading (typing variant: "小伙伴正在回复" to screen readers), empty (no turns: empty hint in the log, FRAME-001).
- **Content limits:** your turn up to 500 characters; a reply up to 120 characters.
- **Overflow:** text wraps; long unbroken strings break anywhere. Only the latest reply carries `data-testid=reply`.

### CMP-003 — Talk button
- **Variants:** laptop full width with icon and "按住说话"; phone 124×48.
- **Sizes:** 56 px tall laptop, 48 px phone.
- **States:** default, hover (pressed tone), focus (Cocoa 3 px outline, 2 px offset), active = listening, disabled = paused:
  - listening, laptop: "在听… N 秒", level bars, "松开发送 · 移开或 Esc 取消".
  - listening, phone: mic icon, "N 秒", level bars only (FRAME-011).
  - listening, accessible name on both: "在听，N 秒，松开发送".
  - paused: while a reply is pending (50 % opacity, ≤ 1 s) — duration retired, increment 1 (until the reply or a code).
- **Content limits:** label 4 characters; seconds up to 60 — retired, increment 1 (English label, CMP-003 increment 1).
- **Overflow:** on phone the release and cancel words are dropped from the button (92 px of content at "60 秒" in 124 px); the label "按住说话" already says to hold, and Esc still cancels.

### CMP-004 — Text input
- **Variants:** one; `data-testid=fake-input`; placeholder "打字也可以，Enter 发送".
- **Sizes:** 48 px tall; laptop full width, phone fills the row beside the talk button.
- **States:** default, focus (focused at open, Cocoa 2 px edge), filled, paused while a reply is pending (stays editable and keeps focus and text; only Enter is ignored; no fade, not `disabled`; note "(发送中，文字先留着)" after the text, FRAME-010), error (none: an empty Enter does nothing).
- **Content limits:** 500 characters.
- **Overflow:** one line; scrolls sideways inside the field.

### CMP-005 — Hint line
- **Variants:** permission-prompt (mic icon), refused, no-mic, busy (mic-off icon), too-short (info icon). Words from `01-ux.md` §4.
- **Sizes:** fills the composer width.
- **States:** default (visible), empty (hidden: no space reserved), focus (not focusable; `role=status`, read once when it appears), error (no-mic, busy).
- **Content limits:** one sentence, ≤ 30 characters — retired, increment 1 (CMP-005 increment 1).
- **Overflow:** wraps to two lines on phone (FRAME-008).

## 6. Accessibility rules
Ratios computed from the palette (WCAG relative luminance, alpha flattened on Oat or Linen).

| Pair or element | Rule | Value |
|---|---|---|

| text.primary on turn.you-bg | ≥ 4.5:1 | 10.6:1 |
| text.muted on surface.page | ≥ 4.5:1 | 5.5:1 |
| text.muted on turn.you-bg (voice tag) | ≥ 4.5:1 | 5.0:1 |
| text.on-action on action.primary | ≥ 4.5:1 | 5.3:1 |
| text.on-action on action.pressed | ≥ 4.5:1 | 6.7:1 |
| border.control on surface.page | ≥ 3:1 (control edge) | 3.4:1 |
| hint.icon on surface.page | ≥ 3:1 (icon) | 4.4:1 |
| companion.line on every mood glow | ≥ 3:1 (character outline) | 7.2:1 or more (lowest: unpleasant-high, Tide 46 % on Oat; D-004) |
| focus.ring on surface.page | ≥ 3:1, 3 px outline, 2 px offset | 13.0:1 |
| turn.reply-rule | decorative; speaker is also told by typeface and alignment | n/a |
| Talk button, input | target ≥ 44×44 px | 56 / 48 px tall |
| Mood | never colour alone: face, caption and accessible name change | CMP-001 |
| Status (hints, typing) | announced as text: `role=status`, log is `role=log` | CMP-002, CMP-005 |
| Paused talk button | disabled, `aria-disabled`; label stays readable | 50 % opacity ≤ 1 s — duration retired, increment 1 |

## 7. Motion
All loops pause when the tab is hidden. Reduce motion: `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

| Where | What moves | Duration | Reduced-motion alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Companion, neutral | `body` breathes 1→1.02; `eyes` blink (scaleY 1→0.1); `ear-left`, `ear-right` twitch 8°; `tail` sways ±8° | 4 s loop; blink 120 ms every 4–6 s; twitch every ~8 s; tail 3 s | still pose and face |
| Companion, pleasant-high | `character` hops twice −20 px, then bounces −8 px; `arm-left`, `arm-right` wave ±12°, alternating; `tail` wags ±15°; `sparkle-1`…`5` twinkle (scale 0.6→1, fade), staggered | 360 ms per hop; bounce every 1.2 s; wave 600 ms; wag 400 ms; twinkle 900 ms | still jump pose, laugh, sparkles |
| Companion, pleasant-low | `character` sways ±3° about its base; `tail` tip flicks ±10°; `heart` rises 10 px and fades, then returns; `blush` opacity 0.5↔0.7 | 3.2 s loop; flick every 2.5 s; heart 4 s; blush 3.2 s | still lean; heart stays |
| Companion, unpleasant-high | `character` fidgets ±2 px in bursts of three; `tremble` flashes with each burst; `sweat-1` slides 6 px, `sweat-2` 1 s later; pupils glance ±2 px; clasped paws rub ±1.5 px | 180 ms each, a burst every 1.6 s; 2 s drop loop; glance every 2.4 s | still; sweat and tremble marks stay |
| Companion, unpleasant-low | `character` sinks 8 px and squashes to scaleY 0.97, then breathes slowly; `tear` slides 10 px down the cheek, fades, returns; `ears` droop 3° more; `eyes` blink slowly | 600 ms settle; 5 s breath; tear 3 s; ears 5 s; blink 300 ms every 6 s | still; tear stays |
| Companion part pivots | arms at the shoulder (path start), ears at their base, tail at the body end, `character` at its bottom centre; use `transform-box: fill-box` with these origins | n/a | n/a |
| Mood change (on reply) | expression crossfade, glow colour, one 0.96→1 squash | 240 ms, 600 ms, 150 ms | instant swap of face and glow |
| Typing dots | dots fade in turn | 1.2 s loop | static "…" |
| New turn | fades and rises 8 px; log scrolls to newest | 200 ms | appears at once; jump scroll |
| Talk button, listening | level bars follow input level; seconds tick | live | bars static; seconds still tick |
| Hint line | fades in | 150 ms | appears at once |

### Components (increment 1)

#### CMP-006 — Language switch
- **Variants:** Selected = zh, en (each option written in its own language: `中文`, `EN`). Figma: set 27:568 on FRAME-016.
- **Sizes:** 98 × 36 px on both sizes: 1 px `color.border.control` outline, `radius.control`, 1 px inner padding, 2 px gap; each option 32 px tall, 12 px side padding, 4 px radius (`中文` 50 px, `EN` 42 px wide). Text `ui/small` 13/20.
- **Placement:** top-right of the companion stage, above the art: laptop 24 px from the stage's top and right edges (FRAME-012, FRAME-014), phone 8 px (FRAME-013, FRAME-015). First in page order.
- **States:** default, hover, focus (Cocoa 3 px ring, 2 px offset, FRAME-016 "EN focused"), active = selected; no disabled, loading, error or empty:
  - default: current option on a `color.turn.you-bg` fill in 600 `color.text.primary`; the other option unfilled in 400 `color.text.muted`.
  - hover (the other option): its text turns `color.text.primary`; the current option has no hover.
  - focus: `color.focus.ring` 3 px ring, 2 px offset, on the focused option (`:focus-visible`); after a switch, focus stays on the pressed option, now current.
  - active = selected: `aria-pressed=true` on the current option.
  - disabled, loading, error, empty: none; the switch is client-only and always works, also while a request is pending.
- **Behaviour:**
  - `role=group` named `langLabel` (界面语言 / Interface language).
  - Two `button`s, each with its own `lang` (`zh-Hans`, `en`); accessible names "中文" and "English" (`langZh` / `langEn`).
  - Click, Enter or Space on the other option switches every interface word at once (no motion, no toast).
  - On the current option it does nothing.
- **Content limits:** fixed: two options, these words.
- **Overflow:** none; fixed width.

#### CMP-002 — Turn (increment 1)
- **you-voice:** tag is the mic icon plus `voiceTag` ("语音" / "Voice").
- **you-transcribing (new variant):** drawn on FRAME-012 and FRAME-016.
  - Your turn box with the voice tag; body `transcribing` ("正在转成文字…" / "Turning it into text…") in `color.text.muted`.
  - The trailing "…" is three dots that fade in turn like the typing dots.
  - Words arrive: the same box swaps its body to them in `color.text.primary`, in place, with no new turn.
  - Nothing heard or a code: the box leaves the log.
- **Content limits:** a reply up to 120 characters in Chinese, 40 words in English.

#### CMP-001 — Companion (increment 1)
- **Caption:**
  - English: "Little Buddy · <phrase>", at most 26 characters after the dot; never wraps.
